### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm a final-year **Computer Science & Engineering** student from **Chattogram, Bangladesh**. I care less about making models bigger and more about understanding **what's actually going on inside them**.

I'm the kind of person who reads a paper, gets curious about one weird detail, and three days later has a notebook full of activation plots. I like problems that are real, a little messy, and haven't been solved the obvious way yet.

<br>

### 🔬 What I'm into

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

**🧠 Mechanistic interpretability**<br>
Circuits, features, attention heads and probing: reverse-engineering what a network has learned instead of just trusting it.

**🔍 Explainability**<br>
Attribution, saliency and making model decisions legible to the humans who have to live with them.

**🛡️ AI security**<br>
Adversarial attacks, jailbreaks, prompt injection and data poisoning. How models break, and how to make them harder to break.

</td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">

**⚡ Quantization & efficient inference**<br>
Squeezing big models onto small hardware: low-bit formats, local inference and getting more out of every GB of VRAM.

**💬 LLMs & NLP**<br>
Transformers, language models and speech, and how they behave once they leave the benchmark.

**🎮 Reinforcement learning**<br>
Agents that learn by doing, and all the creative ways they find to cheat the reward.
